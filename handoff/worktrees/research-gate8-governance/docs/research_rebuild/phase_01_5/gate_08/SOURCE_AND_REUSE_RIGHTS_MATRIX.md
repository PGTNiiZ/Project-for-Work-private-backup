## Approval rule

For each source and operation (access, transform, analyze, retain, share, publish), record the applicable documentary basis, scope, decision-maker, date, and conditions. Unknown or absent evidence remains **NOT VERIFIED**; it is not a negative legal finding and cannot be converted to approval by inference. Scientific execution and release remain **PENDING HUMAN APPROVAL** until the applicable evidence and explicit approval are documented. Preserve the no-raw-publication safeguards in `DATA_GOVERNANCE_ASSESSMENT.md`.
