# Gate 8 — Human Approval Checklist

**Current disposition: PENDING HUMAN APPROVAL.** No item is marked approved merely because repository files or prior processing exist. Attach or reference documentary evidence; record decision-maker, date, scope, and conditions. A checked item without evidence is not clearance.

## A. Data owner / custodian

- [ ] Identify the accountable data owner/custodian and their authority to approve this use.
- [ ] Identify exact source(s), revisions, snapshots, derivatives, labels, embeddings, images, and artifacts in scope; document provenance and unresolved gaps.
- [ ] Provide documentary permission for the proposed access, transformation, research use, retention, and any sharing; identify restrictions and expiry.
- [ ] Confirm approved data location, authorized personnel, access controls, retention duration, deletion method, and incident contact.
- [ ] Explicitly approve or reject the specific proposed scientific analysis; do not treat Gate 8 documentation as a run authorization.

## B. Legal / privacy / platform review

- [ ] Review applicable license and derivative/reuse terms for each source and software/model component.
- [ ] Determine whether platform terms/policies and collection circumstances permit the proposed processing and retention.
- [ ] Determine applicable privacy/data-protection obligations and the permitted basis/purpose; do not infer consent or another basis.
- [ ] Review any proposed transfer, external service, vendor, or cross-border processing. Until approved, none is authorized.
- [ ] Decide whether any output can be shared or published and under what documented conditions.

## C. Ethics / institutional review

- [ ] Research lead describes purpose, methods, population, risks, data minimization, and intended outputs.
- [ ] Appropriate institutional ethics authority documents whether review is required and, if required, provides the applicable determination/approval before execution.
- [ ] Record approval/ determination identifier, date, scope, conditions, and responsible authority. Do not infer IRB approval or exemption from silence or prior repository activity.
- [ ] Confirm any required consent/notice or other safeguards with the authorized ethics/legal authority; do not infer that public profiles imply consent.

## D. Privacy and publication safeguards

- [ ] Keep raw profiles, names, usernames, bios, locations, URLs/images, linkable IDs, pair rows, and row-level features/predictions/decisions/errors out of public materials by default.
- [ ] Review aggregates, figures, examples, manifests, notebooks, and supplementary files for small-cell disclosure, re-identification, linkage, differencing, and raw-data leakage.
- [ ] Confirm any code, license, model, or third-party material obligations; inspect exact release artifacts, not just descriptions.
- [ ] Approve a specific release package and recipient/channel, or explicitly prohibit release. Preserve the no-raw-publication safeguards unless a separately documented exceptional authorization is legally and ethically reviewed.

## E. Final recorded decision

- [ ] All applicable documentary evidence is linked or archived in the approved location.
- [ ] Data owner, research lead, legal/privacy reviewer, and ethics authority have recorded decisions within their respective authority.
- [ ] Any conditions, exclusions, retention/deletion date, and re-review triggers are recorded.
- [ ] Final Gate 8 disposition explicitly states **APPROVED WITH SCOPE AND CONDITIONS** or **NOT APPROVED**; otherwise it remains **PENDING HUMAN APPROVAL**.
- [ ] Separate authorization is recorded before scientific execution and before any external transfer or publication.
