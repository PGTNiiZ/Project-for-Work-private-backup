# Claim protocol (frozen before results)

- If B3 > B2 consistently across the three outer entity-disjoint splits under corrected features, sealed labels, identical policy class, objective, and realized evaluation budget, a GA-specific optimization benefit may be discussed for this corpus and objective.
- If B3 ≈ B2, attribute improvement to validation-selected expanded policy, not specifically to GA. GA is one implementation mechanism.
- If B2 > B3, GA must not be the headline contribution; report it as weaker than the non-GA optimizer in this setting.

These rules are fixed before inspecting sealed-test results. They do not authorize claims of universal GA superiority, deployment benefit, or business cost reduction.
