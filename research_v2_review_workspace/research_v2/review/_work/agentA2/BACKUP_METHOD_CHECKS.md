## Assumption tests

### (a) "GA was only compared against a manual threshold"
- **ICSEC v1 / historical (F1a): TRUE.** Methods are GB-17, GB-18 (fixed 0.98/0.95) and GB-18-GA only [BK1:experiments/code/run_icsec_repeated_outer.py#L49]; R-series compares GA against `R0_production` / `R2_manual` baselines [BK1:experiments/code/run_ga_experiments.py#L102-L103,L129-L130].
- **ICSEC v2/v2r1 (F1b): FALSE.** Baselines are B0 fixed, B1 Sobol over the two-threshold class (12,000 calls), and B2 Sobol over the identical four-parameter class with exactly the B3 realised budget of 12,000 calls [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/authoritative_run_v2/BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md#L5-L12]; [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/authoritative_run_v2/final_runner.py#L184-L187]; fairness passed [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/authoritative_run_v2r1/scientific_outputs/outer_101/fairness.json]. Result: B3 U 1858.2 vs B2 1860.0 (difference 1.8, inside the SD), so no GA superiority under the pre-registered claim rule [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/authoritative_run_v2/CLAIM_PROTOCOL.md#L3-L5].
- **Reviewer revision (F2):** adds the closed-form Chow rule, with no search (U 1879.0) [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/reviewer_revision/aggregate.json#Chow].
- **Not present anywhere executed:** plain random search, grid search, TPE/Bayesian optimisation, coordinate search. `git grep` found no such optimiser in `experiments/code`, `experiments/icsec_final`, or `src` (only Sobol). Gate 9 proposes P3 random, P4 scrambled Sobol and P5 GA at 2,048 calls each, but it is DRAFT_NOT_APPROVED and not executed [BK1:handoff/worktrees/research-phase15-integration/docs/research_rebuild/phase_01_5/gate_09/EXPERIMENT_CONFIG_V1.json#stages.E].
- **Feature-aware GA (F3):** comparators (score-only 2-threshold, legacy name guard) are also GA-optimised, with unequal budgets. No non-GA optimiser.

### (b) Is the ICSEC headline candidate-level or end-to-end?
- **Workflow-level, including blocking misses.** Workflow = exact-tier + model-scored + BLOCKING_MISS rows, and misses are added as `actual=1, decision=NO_MATCH` (FN) [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/authoritative_run_v2/final_runner.py#L246-L264]. The v1 consistency report includes misses in workflow rows [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/authoritative_run/AUTHORITATIVE_RESULTS.md#L59-L63].
- **However,** the headline "decision recall/F1" excludes positive REVIEW from the denominator [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/authoritative_run_v2r1/PROTOCOL_V2R1.md#L77-L83]. It is end-to-end over candidate generation, but conditional on non-deferred decisions. The reviewer revision adds automatic end-to-end recall/F1 counting positive REVIEW (B3: decision F1 0.8903 vs end-to-end F1 0.8610) [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/reviewer_revision/aggregate.json#B3].
- Scope is still role-local: ungenerated negatives and cross-role pairs are not evaluated [BK1:paper/reviewer_revision/main.tex#L161].

### (c) Was the base model trained on data whose entities overlap the GA test split?
- **ICSEC v2/v2r1: NO.** GB-17/18 and the calibrators are refit per outer split on TRAIN/CALIBRATION roles only, and entity overlap with SEALED_TEST is 0 [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/authoritative_run_v2/final_runner.py#L149-L175]; [BK1:artifacts/audit/icsec_review_20261010/evidence.json#entity_overlap]; [BK1:experiments/icsec_final/authoritative_run_v2/scientific_inputs/outer_101/input_artifact_manifest.json#disjointness]. MiniLM is pretrained and receives no labels.
- **ICSEC v1 / historical: PARTIAL YES (indirect).** GB models were refit on model_train, but the `name_sim` feature was gated by the stage-15 predecessor score [BK1:experiments/code/exp_lib.py#L93-L101], and that scorer's training split is unverifiable. So test-entity information may enter through the feature.
- **Feature-aware GA full-cache runs (F3) and R-series: UNKNOWN / likely yes.** The policy input `probability` is the stage-15 CRM model score [BK1:experiments/code/run_full_feature_aware_ga_pipeline.py#L228]; [BK1:experiments/code/exp_lib.py#L19,L78], and the GA val/test split was imposed afterwards [BK1:experiments/code/exp_lib.py#L86-L91]. No record shows the CRM model's training entities.
- **F3 labelled-population runs:** `probability` comes from the multimodal scorer trained on the same train_base split (train/val/test by profile_id), so the GA val/test entities were not in its training split. Its TF-IDF was fit transductively [BK1:src/identity_resolution/stages/train_base.py#L308-L311].
